DECK: Discipleship Experience (Section 7) Bible Experience I

Q1. According to Biblical Interpretation, the defining quality God prioritizes in those entrusted to pass on doctrine is:
A. Charisma
B. Anointing
C. Faithfulness
D. Eloquence
ANSWER: C

Q2. Final authority of Scripture means that the Word of God has:
A. The last word on all matters of faith, doctrine and practice
B. The final word wherever questions of right and wrong arise
C. Supreme authority over doctrine and the believer's spiritual life
D. Ultimate authority whenever human reasoning reaches its limits
ANSWER: A

Q3. According to the lesson on hermeneutics, the apostles and prophets were given the responsibility of:
A. Creating new doctrine based on their personal encounters with God
B. Translating Old Testament prophecy into culturally relevant Christian teaching
C. Establishing which Old Testament passages should remain authoritative
D. Receiving revelation and laying the foundation of doctrine
ANSWER: D

Q4. The statement "Doctrine is not your invention" primarily means that doctrine:
A. Is established independently of the individual who encounters it.
B. Is inherited from the church and therefore should not be questioned.
C. Is determined by the interpretation of Scripture already accepted by believers.
D. Is established before an individual studies Scripture and therefore requires no interpretation.
ANSWER: A

Q5. A believer who has become established in fundamental doctrine reaches a stage where Bible study should increasingly help them:
A. Discover increasingly radical interpretations of familiar passages
B. Move from personal study into teaching ministry
C. Replace foundational teaching with deeper and more advanced revelations
D. Appreciate what they have always known rather than constantly searching for what is new
ANSWER: D

Q6. Which of the following is the Greek word for revelation?
A. Rhema
B. Logos
C. Gnosis
D. Apocalypsis
ANSWER: D

Q7. In Having a Biblical Worldview, "All Scripture is given by inspiration of God" establishes primarily that:
A. The human writers were moved by God in the process of writing Scripture.
B. Scripture communicates God's will through the experiences of its writers.
C. God is the source of all Scripture.
D. The content of Scripture ultimately reflects God's intention through human authorship.
ANSWER: C

Q8. According to the lesson on Having a Biblical Worldview, what should happen when a believer discovers that an ideology they hold is wrong?
A. The Word should straighten the ideology rather than the believer searching for a church that reinforces it.
B. The believer should retain the conviction until they receive a clearer personal revelation.
C. The believer should consider whether the ideology is culturally acceptable before changing it.
D. The believer should compare the ideology with the prevailing interpretation within their church.
ANSWER: A

Q9. The Greek word musterion in Ephesians 3, is explained in the lesson as referring to:
A. A truth that can only be understood by spiritually mature believers
B. A truth deliberately withheld because God had not yet decided to reveal it
C. A supernatural truth that remains inaccessible through ordinary reading
D. Something concealed that is now revealed
ANSWER: D

Q10. The Latin expression for faith alone is:
A. Sola Scriptura
B. Sola Gratia
C. Soli Deo Gloria
D. Sola Fide
ANSWER: D

Q11. The phrase "retain it in its originality and hand it over to people who will hand it over to others" is presented as the practical evidence of:
A. Faithfulness to an orthodox faith that is not subject to personal invention
B. Spiritual maturity demonstrated through the ability to reproduce one's interpretation
C. Apostolic authority demonstrated through successive generations of teachers
D. Accurate Bible study demonstrated through the ability to explain Scripture creatively
ANSWER: A

Q12. The lesson on hermeneutics describes Christ as "the most important piece in Bible interpretation." What does this mean within the specific argument being made?
A. Every biblical passage must contain the literal name "Jesus" before it can be interpreted
B. Christ provides the interpretive key for understanding the Old Testament's types, shadows and symbols
C. The New Testament should be studied independently of the Old Testament because Christ replaced its message
D. Every Old Testament passage should be interpreted exclusively as a direct prediction of Jesus' earthly ministry
ANSWER: B

Q13. In Having a Biblical Worldview, the word "so" in John 3:16 is explained as primarily communicating:
A. The intensity with which God loved the world
B. The magnitude of God's love for humanity
C. The depth of God's emotional affection toward the world
D. The manner in which God demonstrated His love
ANSWER: D

Q14. God's plan for the church, as presented in the lesson on Bible Interpretation, is that believers:
A. Become sufficiently grounded to recognize sound teaching when they hear it
B. Come into the unanimity of the faith with accuracy and clarity
C. Become increasingly dependent on the teaching ministry of their pastor
D. Develop enough personal revelation to contribute meaningfully when they gather
ANSWER: B

Q15. Why does the lesson on Having a Biblical Worldview connect Paul's statement about failing to provide for one's relatives with denying the faith?
A. Because Christian families have a specific obligation to financially support one another
B. Because practical care is one of the strongest ways believers demonstrate their righteousness.
C. Because certain conduct is so inconsistent with professed faith that its absence can contradict the profession itself.
D. Because failure to care for relatives demonstrates that the believer has misunderstood the doctrine of grace.
ANSWER: C

Q16. What is the strongest indication that biblical interpretation is being done correctly?
A. The believer becomes more confident when defending doctrine
B. The believer develops greater knowledge of historical and theological details
C. The believer becomes increasingly skilled at identifying erroneous interpretations
D. The knowledge drives the believer toward worship and greater response to God
ANSWER: D

Q17. What does "where the Spirit of the Lord is, there is liberty" mean in its immediate interpretive context?
A. Freedom to express oneself physically during church services
B. Freedom from the veil so that believers can behold the glory and intent of God's writings
C. Freedom from the need to interpret Scripture according to its original context
D. Freedom from the authority of the Old Testament because the Spirit has replaced Scripture
ANSWER: B

Q18. The lesson on hermeneutics uses the description of Christ as "the express image of his person" to emphasize that:
A. Christ's teachings are more practical than those of the prophets
B. Christ represents the cultural fulfillment of Old Testament prophecy
C. Christ is the final prophet through whom the Old Testament can be interpreted
D. In Christ, we understand exactly who God is
ANSWER: D

Q19. Which pair correctly matches the biblical statement with the figure of speech identified in the hermeneutics lesson?
A. "I am the door" - simile
B. "Behold, I send you forth as sheep" - metaphor
C. "I am the vine" - hyperbole
D. "I send you forth as sheep in the midst of wolves" - simile
ANSWER: D

Q20. Which combination accurately reflects the languages identified in the lesson?
A. Old Testament - Hebrew and Aramaic; New Testament - Koine Greek
B. Old Testament - Hebrew and Greek; New Testament - Aramaic and Greek
C. Old Testament - Hebrew only; New Testament - Koine Greek and Latin
D. Old Testament - Aramaic and Latin; New Testament - Hebrew and Koine Greek
ANSWER: A

Q21. Stephen's description of his Jewish audience as "uncircumcised in the heart" is used in the lesson on hermeneutics to establish what distinction?
A. Physical circumcision could exist without the spiritual reality it was intended to signify
B. Physical circumcision was never actually commanded by God
C. The Jews had abandoned the practice of circumcision before Stephen addressed them
D. Circumcision of the heart was exclusively a New Testament doctrine with no Old Testament precedent
ANSWER: A

Q22. Which interpretation does the lesson on Hermeneutics explicitly reject concerning the statement "the letter kills"?
A. That "the letter" specifically refers to the law written on stones
B. That the statement refers to the law in contrast with the ministry of the Spirit
C. That "the letter" means the entire Bible is incapable of giving life without a personal rhema
D. That Paul was contrasting the ministry associated with the law with the ministry of the New Testament
ANSWER: C

Q23. Which statement most accurately distinguishes "sundry times" from "diverse manners" according to the lesson?
A. Sundry times concerns different prophets, while diverse manners concerns different generations
B. Sundry times concerns prophecy, while diverse manners concerns Scripture
C. Sundry times refers to communication in many different parts, while diverse manners refers to different modes of communication
D. Sundry times refers to Old Testament communication, while diverse manners refers exclusively to New Testament communication
ANSWER: C

Q24. The instruction to read "per book, per chapter" is specifically intended to prevent the interpreter from:
A. Relying on individual verses instead of the full counsel of Scripture
B. Removing a text from its context and assigning meaning based on the words alone
C. Reading Scripture according to personal experiences and previously held convictions
D. Reading Scripture according to personal experiences and previously held convictions
ANSWER: B

Q25. Romans 16:16 is primarily an example of which interpretation barrier according to the lesson on Biblical Interpretation?
A. Translation
B. Language
C. Culture
D. Doctrine
ANSWER: C

Q26. According to the hermeneutics lesson, why can an honest and careful translation still make Bible interpretation difficult?
A. Translators inevitably change the doctrines of the original writers
B. Biblical languages cannot adequately express spiritual realities in English
C. English translations necessarily remove figures of speech from the text
D. Some meaning and nuance can become less immediately clear through translation
ANSWER: D

Q27. Which text does the lesson on Bible Interpretation identify as having had an exceptionally significant impact on world history?
A. John 3:16
B. John 1:1
C. 2 Corinthians 2:11
D. Galatians 3:11
ANSWER: D

Q28. In Having a Biblical Worldview, "Your word is a lamp unto my feet and a light unto my path" was used to mean:
A. Scripture gives believers insight into God's specific plan for their future.
B. Scripture orders the believer's steps and determines how they navigate life.
C. Scripture provides principles by which believers can evaluate their circumstances.
D. Scripture gives believers sufficient wisdom to make decisions without external counsel.
ANSWER: B

Q29. Which combination best reflects the progression identified in Isaiah 53 within the lesson on hermeneutics?
A. Christ's incarnation -> earthly ministry -> ascension -> enthronement
B. Christ's rejection and suffering -> bearing of sin -> death -> resurrection implied
C. Israel's rejection -> restoration -> establishment of the church -> final judgment
D. The law -> the prophets -> the gospel -> justification by works
ANSWER: B

Q30. The biblical worldview becomes evident when the Word of God becomes the believer's:
A. Standard for evaluating spiritual experiences
B. Foundation for establishing doctrinal convictions
C. Guide for making decisions concerning Christian practice
D. Lens through which they see the world
ANSWER: D
